### User Goal

The student wants to report a lost item so that it can be recorded and potentially matched with an item reported as found.

### Flow

**START**
↓
**Open Application**
↓
**Login**
↓
**Home Screen**
↓
**Select "Report Lost Item"**
↓
**Enter Item Details**
↓
**Select Item Category**
↓
**Enter Location Lost**
↓
**Enter Date/Time Lost**
↓
**Upload Item Photo**
↓
**Review Information**
↓
**Submit Lost Item Report**
↓
**Confirmation Message**
↓
**Receive Notifications About Updates**
↓
**END**

### Expected Outcome

The lost item is successfully reported and the student can receive updates about possible matches.

# Task Flow 3 — Search and Claim an Item

### User Goal

The student wants to search for a lost item, identify a possible match, and submit a claim.

### Flow

**START**
↓
**Open Application**
↓
**Login**
↓
**Home Screen**
↓
**Select "Search"**
↓
**Enter Item Name/Keyword**
↓
**Select Category**
↓
**Select Location**
↓
**View Search Results**
↓
**Select Possible Matching Item**
↓
**View Item Details**
↓
**Is This My Item?**

**NO** → Return to Search Results

**YES**
↓
**Select "Claim Item"**
↓
**Enter Claim Details**
↓
**Submit Claim**
↓
**Claim Confirmation**
↓
**Track Claim Status**
↓
**Receive Notification About Claim Update**
↓
**END**

### Expected Outcome

The student successfully submits a claim for a potentially matching item and can track the progress of the claim.

---

# Relationship to User Research

The task flows were developed from the findings of the student user research. The research identified the lack of a centralized reporting system as the main challenge and showed strong demand for reporting lost and found items, photo uploads, notifications, search functionality and claim tracking.

The research also showed that students wanted the application to be simple and intuitive, with all five participants rating the desired ease of use at 5 out of 5.
