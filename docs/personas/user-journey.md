# USER JOURNEY — STUDENT RECOVERING A LOST ITEM

## Persona

**Persona:** Brian Mwangi — Student Who Lost an Item

## Scenario

Brian realizes that he has lost his scientific calculator somewhere on campus. He wants to find out whether another student has reported finding it and, if necessary, report the calculator as lost and track any possible claim or match.

| Stage                       | User Action                                                                                   | User Thought                                  | Emotion      | Pain Point                                                       | Design Opportunity                                     |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. Realizes Item Is Missing | Brian notices that his scientific calculator is missing.                                      | "Where did I lose my calculator?"             | 😟 Worried   | He does not know where to begin searching.                       | Provide a clear and accessible Lost-and-Found service. |
| 2. Opens the App            | Brian opens the Student Lost-and-Found Mobile Application.                                    | "Maybe someone has found it."                 | 🤔 Hopeful   | He needs to immediately understand what he can do.               | Keep the interface simple and intuitive.               |
| 3. Searches for Item        | Brian searches for a scientific calculator and can use category or location information.      | "Has someone already reported my calculator?" | 🔎 Hopeful   | Searching through informal channels can be difficult and slow.   | Provide centralized search and filtering.              |
| 4. No Match Found           | Brian does not immediately find a matching item.                                              | "I should report it as lost."                 | 😐 Concerned | There is no match yet.                                           | Provide a prominent "Report Lost Item" option.         |
| 5. Reports Lost Item        | Brian enters the item's details, location and other relevant information and uploads a photo. | "Now people can identify it if they find it." | 🙂 Relieved  | Reporting should not be complicated.                             | Use a short, simple reporting form with photo upload.  |
| 6. Receives Notification    | Brian receives a notification about a possible matching found item.                           | "Someone may have found my calculator!"       | 😊 Excited   | Without notifications, he would need to keep checking manually.  | Provide timely notifications about relevant updates.   |
| 7. Views Possible Match     | Brian opens the notification and views the reported item's details and photo.                 | "This looks like mine."                       | 🤞 Hopeful   | He needs enough information to determine whether it is his item. | Display clear item details and images.                 |
| 8. Submits Claim            | Brian submits a claim for the item.                                                           | "I hope my claim is accepted."                | 😬 Anxious   | He needs to know what happens after submitting a claim.          | Provide a clear claim process and confirmation.        |
| 9. Tracks Claim             | Brian checks the status of his claim.                                                         | "Has my claim been reviewed?"                 | 🙂 Reassured | Waiting without updates creates uncertainty.                     | Provide claim tracking and notifications.              |
| 10. Recovers Item           | Brian successfully recovers his calculator.                                                   | "I finally got it back!"                      | 😄 Happy     | The recovery process should be clear and convenient.             | Provide clear completion and recovery instructions.    |

## Key UX Opportunities

The journey highlights the need for:

1. A simple and intuitive interface.
2. A centralized reporting system.
3. Easy lost-item reporting.
4. Photo uploads.
5. Search by category and location.
6. Timely notifications.
7. Clear item information.
8. A simple claim process.
9. Claim tracking.
