# USER PERSONA 1 — THE STUDENT WHO LOST AN ITEM

## Personal Information

**Name:** Brian Mwangi
**Age:** 21
**Year of Study:** 3rd Year
**Course:** Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

### Background

Brian is a university student who regularly moves between lecture halls, laboratories, the library, and other areas around campus. Because he carries several personal and academic items throughout the day, he is concerned about losing important belongings.

He has previously lost a personal item on campus and had to rely on classmates, WhatsApp groups, or the campus security office to try to recover it.

### Goals

* Quickly report a lost item.
* Search for items that have been found on campus.
* Upload a photo to help identify the lost item.
* Receive notifications when a possible match is found.
* Track the progress of a claim.

### Frustrations

* There is no centralized place for reporting lost items.
* It can be difficult to know where to report a lost item.
* WhatsApp groups are not organized specifically for lost-and-found items.
* Communication about lost items can be slow.
* Finding the owner or locating a lost item can be difficult.

### Needs

* A simple and intuitive mobile interface.
* A clear "Report Lost Item" option.
* Search and filtering by category and location.
* Photo upload functionality.
* Notifications about possible matches.
* Claim tracking.

### Technology Behaviour

Brian frequently uses his smartphone for communication and university-related activities. He prefers a simple process that allows him to report an item quickly without completing unnecessary steps.

### Scenario

Brian realizes that he has lost his scientific calculator after leaving a laboratory. Instead of asking several classmates or posting in different WhatsApp groups, he opens the Student Lost-and-Found Mobile Application, reports the lost calculator, uploads its photo, provides the location where he last had it, and waits for a notification if someone reports a matching found item.
