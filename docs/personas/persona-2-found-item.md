# USER PERSONA 2 — THE STUDENT WHO FINDS AN ITEM

## Personal Information

**Name:** Sharon Wanjiku
**Age:** 22
**Year of Study:** 4th Year
**Course:** Bachelor of Business Information Technology

### Background

Sharon regularly moves around campus between classes, the library, and other university facilities. She is willing to help other students who lose their belongings, but she may not always know the correct way to report an item she has found.

### Goals

* Easily report a found item.
* Upload a photo of the found item.
* Provide the location where the item was found.
* Help the owner identify and recover the item.
* Receive notifications when the owner makes a claim.

### Frustrations

* There is no centralized reporting mechanism.
* She may not know where to report a found item.
* Identifying the owner can be difficult.
* Communication with the owner may be slow.
* She may be uncertain about what to do with an item she finds.

### Needs

* A simple "Report Found Item" option.
* A quick reporting process.
* Photo upload functionality.
* Category and location information.
* Clear instructions on what happens after reporting.
* Notifications about claims or possible owners.

### Technology Behaviour

Sharon uses her smartphone regularly and prefers simple, straightforward applications. She wants to report a found item quickly without having to search through WhatsApp groups or personally locate the owner.

### Scenario

Sharon finds a student's USB flash drive near the library. She is unsure who owns it and does not know where to report it. She opens the Student Lost-and-Found Mobile Application, selects "Report Found Item," uploads a photo, enters the location and item details, and submits the report. The owner can then find the listing and submit a claim.
