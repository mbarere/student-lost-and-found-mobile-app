USABILITY TESTING

Usability testing was conducted to evaluate the usability, effectiveness, and overall user experience of the Student Lost-and-Found Application.

The testing involved allowing participants to interact with the application prototype and then complete a questionnaire based on their experience. The questionnaire focused on areas such as ease of navigation, clarity of the interface, reporting lost and found items, searching and filtering, submitting claims, and overall ease of use.
